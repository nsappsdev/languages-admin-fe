# Session notes — 10–11 November 2026

## Sentence audio authoring

- Added a Sentence audio section with automatic sentence segmentation, source-offset-based review and per-sentence readiness.
- Added Split at cursor, Join next, Reset sentence breaks, individual playback and explicit single-sentence regeneration.
- Separated Save text from Prepare sentence audio. Preparation reuses matching sentence recordings; generating missing audio or explicitly regenerating a sentence consumes provider credits.
- Full-text playback previews the narration assembled from sentence recordings. Existing vocabulary preparation continues to reuse or generate separate unknown-word/phrase recordings.
- Unsaved sentence-break edits block publication until applied or reset. Narration requests send reviewed offsets and the expected draft version. The latest failed job remains visible after reopening the workspace.
- Regenerating audio for unchanged source text preserves matching vocabulary selections, translations and saved recording associations through the backend.

Requires the companion backend change and migration `20261110100000_sentence_audio`. Backend/worker rollout must precede use of these controls. Existing published lessons remain immutable.
