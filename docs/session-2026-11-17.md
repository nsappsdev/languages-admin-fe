# Timing recovery — 17 November 2026

Sentence timing failures no longer instruct the admin to regenerate usable
audio. A sentence can offer **Repair timings**, and the text can offer
**Repair saved timings**. Both use the matching backend repair-only job and
refresh the workspace, vocabulary occurrences and publication readiness when
finished. Missing sentence recordings are not synthesized by repair.

Controls are disabled while the draft is unsaved or audio work is active.
An uncertain or failed dispatched repair retains its explanation and Listen
control without automatically repeating the paid request. Existing Generate
and Regenerate actions remain separate.

The backend automatically validates new recordings and upgrades older draft
timings locally. Safe rounding errors are corrected without provider requests;
unusable timing data can be recovered by aligning the existing recording.
Original audio, translations and immutable published versions are preserved.
The backend defaults to a 30% subscription-usage ceiling for alignment requests.
See the companion backend's `AUDIO_AUTHORING.md` for migrations, the existing-text
backfill command, dispatch safety and validation details.
